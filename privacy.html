Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

StreamClips Pipeline ("the app", "we", "our") is a personal automation tool operated by a single individual to repurpose short video clips from Twitch into short-form posts for TikTok, YouTube, and Discord. The app is not offered as a public service and is not available for other users to sign up for. This policy explains what data the app touches and how it is handled.

1. Who operates this app

The app is operated by an individual developer for their own personal and creative use. It connects the operator's own Twitch, TikTok, YouTube, and Discord accounts to one another. It does not collect data from, or act on behalf of, any other person.

2. What data the app accesses

  • Twitch: public clip metadata (clip URLs, titles, view counts, broadcaster names) retrieved via the Twitch API for channels the operator has chosen to follow.
  • TikTok: an OAuth access/refresh token issued by TikTok for the operator's own TikTok account, used solely to publish video posts to that account via the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • YouTube: an OAuth access/refresh token for the operator's own YouTube channel, used solely to upload and schedule videos via the YouTube Data API.
  • Discord: a webhook URL used to post status messages and preview videos to a private Discord channel the operator controls.

The app does not collect data about TikTok viewers, followers, or any third party. It does not use cookies, trackers, or analytics, and it does not access contacts, messages, or any data outside of what is described above.

3. How data is stored and used

OAuth tokens and API credentials are stored locally on the operator's own machine (in a local, non-public configuration file) and are used only to authenticate the app's own API requests. Video files are processed locally and deleted or archived locally after posting. No data collected or generated by the app is sold, rented, or shared with any third party, and none is used for advertising.

4. Third-party services

The app calls the following third-party APIs, each governed by its own privacy policy and terms: Twitch API, TikTok Content Posting API, YouTube Data API (Google), and Discord webhooks. Use of the app's Google integration is limited to the practices described in this policy and complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Data retention and deletion

Access tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the app and can be revoked at any time by the operator directly from the TikTok, YouTube (Google Account), or Discord account settings. Revoking access immediately stops the app from making further API calls on that account. To request deletion of any locally stored data, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or data requests can be sent to mangomoments88@gmail.com.
